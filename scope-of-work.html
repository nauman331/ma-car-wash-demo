Overview

A complete booking and management system for MA Car Wash, delivered in two phases. Phase 1 is the bilingual booking website with automatic WhatsApp booking confirmations and an admin dashboard. Phase 2 adds the driver app, live driver tracking and automated WhatsApp messages at all 5 stages of the booking. MA Car Wash owns the website, the source code, the admin dashboard, the customer database and the whole system.

Phase 1

Booking Website

$500 — 14 days from the day NSolvers receives the packages, prices, logo and photos
  • Bilingual website: Arabic and English, with full RTL Arabic layout.
  • Service packages with editable pricing.
  • Car type selection: Saloon / 4WD.
  • Real-time available booking slots.
  • Booking form: package, car type, date and time slot, location, customer name and WhatsApp number.

Phase 2

Driver App, Live Tracking and WhatsApp Automation

$700 — about 21 days after Phase 1
  • Driver app (on the driver phone): receive bookings; see customer name, address, car details, service and time; follow the map to the customer; update the booking status at each stage (On the way, Arrived, Completed); live location is shared automatically while the driver is on the way.
  • Automated WhatsApp messages at all 5 stages: booking confirmation; driver assigned; driver on the way, including the live tracking link; driver has arrived; car wash service completed.
  • Live tracking link: the customer opens the link in their browser and sees the driver moving on the map in real time, with the location updating continuously while the driver is on the way. No app to download for the customer.
  • Admin dashboard live map: all drivers and vehicles live on a map; all bookings with their live status; assign or change drivers; manage bookings, services and prices.

Customer flow

  1. Customer books on the website and receives an instant WhatsApp booking confirmation.
  2. A driver is assigned and the customer receives a WhatsApp message with the driver details.
  3. When the driver is on the way, the customer receives a WhatsApp message with a live tracking link and follows the driver on the map.
  4. When the driver arrives, the customer receives a WhatsApp message that the driver has arrived.
  5. When the wash is done, the customer receives a WhatsApp message that the service is completed.

Driver flow

  1. The driver receives the booking in the driver app, with customer details and location.
  2. The driver accepts the booking and follows the map to the customer.
  3. The driver updates the status in the app at each stage. Each update automatically sends the customer their WhatsApp message, and the live location is shared while the driver is on the way.

WhatsApp Business API — setup and integration requirements

All automated WhatsApp messages use the official WhatsApp Business API (Cloud API). This connects the booking system to WhatsApp so confirmations and updates are sent automatically, with no manual sending.

What NSolvers does

  • Sets up the official WhatsApp Business API connection for MA Car Wash.
  • Drafts the 5 message templates (booking confirmation, driver assigned, driver on the way with live tracking link, driver arrived, service completed) and submits them to Meta for approval. MA reviews the wording of each template before it is submitted.
  • Connects each booking event to its automated message, and tests every message end to end before delivery.

What is needed from MA Car Wash

  • A WhatsApp Business number for the automated messages. The number used for the API cannot be used in the WhatsApp app on a phone at the same time, so if MA wants to keep chatting with customers on their current number, a separate number is used for the automated messages.
  • Business details for the Meta Business account, if Meta asks for business verification during setup.
  • Review and approval of the wording of the 5 message templates.
Note: message templates must be approved by Meta before they can be sent. NSolvers handles the submission; approval normally takes a short time and does not change the price or the timeline. WhatsApp itself charges a small fee per message sent through the API.
